REJECTION ROOT CAUSES

Recruiter's 5 Red Flags: What Gets Freshers Rejected

Feedback collected from 40+ campus technical interviewers on why promising candidates get disqualified.

01

Listing Technologies You Barely Know

Putting "Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, PyTorch" on your resume after watching a 1-hour overview video. If an interviewer asks a basic deployment question and you blank out, your credibility for everything else is destroyed.

02

Jumping Into Code Without Clarification

In technical rounds, immediately typing code before clarifying constraints (e.g. "Can inputs be negative?", "What is max array size?", "Is memory constrained?") signals poor engineering maturity.

03

Silent Thinking (The Black Box Candidate)

Going completely silent for 8 minutes while scribbling on paper. Interviewers evaluate your thought process, not just the final output. Always vocalize your initial brute-force approach first.

04

Saying "I Don't Have Any Questions"

When the interviewer asks "Do you have any questions for me?" at the end, saying "No, all good" conveys a lack of genuine curiosity. Ask about team deployment cycles or engineering challenges.

05

Defensive Attitude on Code Critiques

If the interviewer points out a bug or suggests an optimization, arguing defensively instead of listening and testing their edge case is an immediate culture fit red flag.
