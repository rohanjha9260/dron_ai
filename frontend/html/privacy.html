1. Statutory Framework & Commitment

Dron-AI Technologies Private Limited ("Dron-AI", "we", "us", or "our") operates an AI/ML-driven student career guidance, placement readiness assessment, and learning roadmap platform for higher education collegiate engineering students in India.

This Privacy Policy is formulated in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023) of India, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

Principle of Data Minimization: Dron-AI operates on the principle that we collect and process only the minimum data strictly necessary to evaluate academic trajectory, calculate career cosine alignment, and predict placement readiness probabilities.

2. Categories of Personal Data Collected

We do not collect sensitive biometrics, Aadhaar numbers, caste/religion details, financial account credentials, or real-time GPS tracking. Below is the comprehensive classification of personal data processed:

3. Purpose Limitation & Machine Learning Inference

Your digital personal data is exclusively utilized for the following specified, lawful purposes:

  • Placement Readiness Scoring: Transforming student vectors through our 13-dimensional canonical feature vector to calculate a placement readiness probability via trained XGBoost models.
  • Career Matching: Calculating cosine similarity between student skill vectors and ideal career vectors (e.g., SDE, Data Scientist, Cloud Architect).
  • Skill Gap Analysis & Roadmaps: Performing mathematical vector subtraction (Ideal Role Vector - Student Vector) to sequence milestone learning plans.
No Automated Adverse Profiling: Dron-AI predictions are purely quantitative advisory guides intended to support self-improvement. They are never sold to external data brokers or used to automatically reject a student from campus recruitment.

4. Lawful Basis & Notice of Consent

Under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data processing by Dron-AI is founded on unconditional, informed, specific, and unambiguous consent obtained via affirmative action (e.g., explicit registration consent checkbox).

Right to Withdraw Consent: You possess the absolute right to withdraw consent at any time via your Settings page or by contacting our Grievance Officer. Upon consent withdrawal, all associated predictive profiles are purged within 30 business days.

5. Storage, Security & Cryptographic Safeguards

In adherence to Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act 2023, Dron-AI implements rigorous reasonable technical and organizational security safeguards:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored. Passwords are salted and hashed using modern multi-round Bcrypt algorithms.
  • Stateless JWT Authentication: Session tokens are cryptographically signed and stored in client-side secure local storage, transmitted strictly over HTTPS/TLS encrypted channels.
  • ML Model Integrity: Serialized machine learning models (`.pkl`) are validated at startup against cryptographic SHA-256 sidecar checksums (`.sha256`), failing closed if any tampering or unauthorized modification is detected.

6. Rights of the Data Principal (Student)

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023, every student possesses enforceable legal rights:

  1. Right to Access: Request a full machine-readable summary of all personal data, academic records, and model prediction history held by Dron-AI.
  2. Right to Correction & Erasure: Rectify inaccurate semester marks or demand permanent erasure of your account and skill vectors.
  3. Right of Grievance Redressal: Submit a complaint regarding data handling to our dedicated Grievance Officer.
  4. Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

7. Third-Party Integrations & CDN Audit

We have conducted a thorough architectural privacy audit:

  • External APIs (GitHub & LeetCode): We query public profiles strictly using the handles you voluntarily provide. We never request private OAuth repository scopes or personal API tokens.
  • Third-Party Advertising Trackers: Dron-AI does NOT contain Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, tracking iframes, or cross-site behavioral advertising trackers.
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): Font Awesome and Chart.js libraries are served via public CDNs for high-availability performance without transmitting personal student data.

8. Age Eligibility & Collegiate Students

The Dron-AI platform is intended for university students, polytechnic scholars, and faculty. In compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023 regarding personal data of children, individuals under the age of 18 must use the platform under verifiable parental or guardian supervision.
