1. Scope of Platform Services

This Cancellation and Refund Policy outlines the terms governing cancellations, service subscriptions, and monetary refunds for services offered by Dron-AI Technologies Private Limited ("Dron-AI").

Our mission is to empower student career readiness through transparent, fair, and consumer-friendly practices compliant with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and e-commerce rules in India.

2. Free Student & Evaluator Dev Mode

The core Dron-AI platform—including student registration, 1-click Dev / Guest mode, academic CGPA regression analysis, career vector recommendations, and baseline placement readiness evaluations—is offered 100% free of charge to collegiate engineering students and faculty evaluators.

Because no monetary fee is assessed for standard student profiles, no charges or cancellations apply to the standard tier.

3. Institutional Subscriptions & Refund Eligibility

For university campus bulk licensing, institutional telemetry dashboards, or premium pro cohort modules, refunds are granted under the following conditions:

  • 14-Day Onboarding Guarantee: If an educational institution decides to cancel a campus deployment within fourteen (14) calendar days of signing the initial subscription agreement, a full refund (less onboarding setup expenses) will be processed.
  • Extended Service Outage: If the Dron-AI cloud infrastructure experiences unannounced continuous downtime exceeding forty-eight (48) hours that prevents student evaluations during critical placement cycles.
  • Duplicate Transactions: Any accidental double-charge or billing processing error will be refunded in full immediately upon verification.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds cannot be issued under the following circumstances:

  • Failure to Achieve Placement: As established in our Terms & Conditions, Dron-AI is an educational predictive analytics tool. Inability to secure employment from corporate recruiters is not grounds for a refund.
  • Requests submitted after the expiration of the 14-day institutional guarantee window.
  • Accounts terminated as a consequence of malicious activity, unauthorized scraping, or violation of our Acceptable Use Policy.

5. Refund Processing & Settlement Timelines

Direct Account Settlement: Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment instrument (Corporate Bank Account / NEFT / RTGS / Payment Gateway) within 5 to 7 business days following written confirmation.
