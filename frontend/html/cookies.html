1. Storage Technologies in Dron-AI

This Cookie & Client Storage Policy explains how Dron-AI Technologies Private Limited utilizes modern browser storage technologies (HTML5 Web Storage, LocalStorage, and HTTP cookies) to provide a seamless, secure experience on our platform.

Unlike traditional advertising platforms that rely on third-party tracking cookies across websites, Dron-AI is built on client-side data minimization. We primarily utilize localStorage to store your authenticated session token, allowing stateless, rapid API communication without transmitting sensitive session data across unrelated domains.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Clean Architecture Commitment: Dron-AI does NOT use Google Ads tracking, Facebook Pixel, double-click cookies, or cross-site fingerprinting technologies. Your academic and career trajectories are never monitored or monetized for commercial advertising.

4. How to Manage and Clear Storage

You have full control over your client-side storage. You can inspect, modify, or erase all stored keys directly from your browser:

  • Google Chrome: Navigate to Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-Party Cookies > See all site data > Search for localhost or dron.ai > Click Delete.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Navigate to Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data > Manage Data.
  • Apple Safari: Navigate to Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • In-Platform Clear: Simply clicking the "Logout" or "Exit Guest Mode" button in the Dron-AI sidebar immediately flushes the dron_ai_token and dron_is_guest keys from your browser memory.
